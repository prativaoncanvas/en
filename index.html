About the Artist
Prativa Debsharma Sarkar

A Bengali watercolour artist, painting between two homes — India and Jacksonville, Florida.

Prativa's work moves quietly between watercolour, acrylic, soft pastel and graphite — a continuing conversation between her Bengali roots and her studio in the American South. Trained in the classical Indian tradition and self-led ever since, her paintings linger on devotional figures from the Hindu pantheon, small domestic moments, and the everyday landscape she keeps falling in love with.

Her illustrations have been featured in the Jagriti publication (2024 & 2025), and her work is held in private collections across India and the United States. She paints daily, takes commissions sparingly, and shares her process with a growing community on YouTube.
